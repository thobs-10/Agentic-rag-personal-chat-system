# Welcome to your AI Assistant 👋

This is a **multi-agent RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** system that intelligently routes your questions to the right specialist.

## What I can do

| Mode | Best for |
|------|----------|
| 🔧 **Technical** | Coding, debugging, algorithms, data science, software architecture |
| 📄 **Personal** | Your personal documents, leases, general knowledge, casual conversation |
| 🤖 **Auto** | Let me decide — I'll classify your query automatically |

## How to use

1. Type your question in the chat box below
2. Use the **⚙️ Settings** icon to choose a mode (or leave it on **auto**)
3. Source documents cited by the assistant appear in the **side panel** on the right

## Tips

- Be specific for better results — mention the language, framework, or document name
- For personal documents, ask directly: *"What does my lease say about pets?"*
- Responses stream token-by-token, just like a real conversation
